### LinkedIn
Every HR manager and business owner in Nigeria knows the monthly spreadsheet panic: calculating graduated PAYE rates, updating pension splits, deducting NHF, and manually formatting bank schedules.

We built Payroll Pro with a clean, high-density minimalist UI to solve this once and for all.

Payroll Pro is an offline-first & cloud-synced payroll operating system built specifically for the Nigerian business reality:
• Complete statutory compliance out of the box (PITA, PRA 2014, NHF, NSITF)
• 1-Click automated salary batch calculations
• Direct commercial bank transfer schedules (NIP standard)
• Full desktop power on Windows + instant on-the-go approvals on Android
• Native performance with zero internet dependency
