# Brag Plan: Payroll Pro

## What is this app?
Payroll Pro is an intelligent, offline-first & cloud-synced payroll operating system built specifically for Nigerian businesses and accountants, automating PAYE, Pension, NHF, NSITF, and 1-click bank transfer schedules.

## The angle
End the end-of-month spreadsheet nightmare. Every Nigerian business owner and HR accountant dreads calculating PAYE brackets, pension splits, and manual bank schedules. Payroll Pro turns hours of stressful calculations into a 1-click automated run with zero compliance penalties.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark, high-contrast visual statement: "End-of-month payroll shouldn't take 3 days of spreadsheet panic." Followed by an instant punchy cut: "What if you could run your entire company's payroll in 1 click?"

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Live Payroll Dashboard: Real-time net pay, gross disbursements, and active staff counts counting up smoothly.
- The 100% Compliant Nigerian Tax Engine: Automated statutory breakdown showing PAYE, 8% Pension, 2.5% NHF, and 1% NSITF verified in milliseconds.
- Direct Bank Payment Schedules & Branded Payslips: Instant NIP-ready bulk payment export and automated employee PDF payslips.

## Outro / punchline
"Payroll sorted in seconds. Pay your people with Payroll Pro."

## User flow worth showing
1. Review monthly staff directory & active salary allowances
2. Click "Execute Payroll Run" — instant batch statutory breakdown
3. Export NIP bank payment schedule & generate all employee payslips
